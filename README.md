# Remix of Birria Brand Blueprint

Absolutely — here’s a clean prompt you can paste into ChatGPT/Claude/etc. to generate the website:

Design and build a modern, minimal website for Birria Pa La Cruda, a Los Angeles/El Sereno-based birria food business.

Brand Direction

Minimal, editorial, slightly raw/street-food aesthetic

Brand colors: black/dark charcoal, grey, and off-white

Use a typewriter-style font prominently for headings, navigation, and labels

Pair it with a clean, readable sans-serif for body text

Use a beautiful El Sereno landscape photo as a major background/hero element

Keep the design spacious and sophisticated without feeling overly corporate

Use high-quality food and event photography throughout

The overall feeling should be authentic, local, artistic, and rooted in Los Angeles food culture

Pages

1. Home

Large El Sereno landscape hero

Birria Pa La Cruda logo/name

Short brand statement

Prominent buttons:

Book Now

Menu

Schedule

Featured food photography

Upcoming event/pop-up section

Instagram/social link

2. About
Tell the story of Birria Pa La Cruda and Chef Carlos Jaquez.
Highlight that the business grew from a Sunday pop-up in El Sereno and is rooted in birria, community, Mexican food traditions, quality ingredients, and Los Angeles culture.

3. Menu
Create a visually appealing menu section featuring their current birria dishes, tacos, quesadillas, consomé, and other offerings.
Do not invent prices or current menu items. Structure the page so the client can easily update the menu later.

4. Schedule
Create a section showing:

Upcoming pop-ups

Current locations

Dates and times

Special events/collaborations

Directions/location links

Make this easy for the client to update because locations and events may change.

5. Portfolio / Gallery
Create a visual archive of their work:

Food photography

Pop-ups

Events

Collaborations

Catering/private events

Make this feel more like an editorial photo archive than a traditional corporate portfolio.

6. Booking / Detailed Inquiry
Create a professional booking questionnaire with fields for:

Name

Email

Phone

Event type

Event date

Guest count

Event location

Budget

Instagram/website

What they're looking for

Additional details/message

Include a clear Submit Inquiry button.

Homepage Copy Direction

Build the messaging around:

El Sereno → birria → community → culture → Los Angeles

The website should communicate that Birria Pa La Cruda is more than just a food business — it is a local LA food experience rooted in community and craft.

Important

Do not fabricate current business information, menu items, prices, locations, or schedules. Use placeholders where information needs to be provided by the client.

The final website should feel minimal, memorable, authentic, photographic, and distinctly Birria Pa La Cruda, rather than looking like a generic restaurant template.
